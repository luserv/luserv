# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers at remote-friendly companies (LatAm-eligible roles). They skim: a few minutes, often on a laptop between other candidates, deciding whether this developer has shipped real, deployed work. Job to be done: verify credibility fast (live links, real stack, clear scope) and decide to make contact.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for a full-stack developer (Go, SvelteKit, PostgreSQL, Next.js) who builds products end to end, from offline-first apps to deployed web systems. It exists to turn a skim into an email. Success: a visitor opens a live project and sends a message.

## Brand Personality

Precise, technical, calm. Confident without shouting; the work carries the claim. Voice is plain and specific: names stacks, dates and statuses instead of adjectives.

## Anti-references

- Template dev portfolios: gradient hero, "Hi, I'm X" plus tech-icon grid, identical project cards.
- Neon cyberpunk or terminal cosplay: matrix green, glitch text, fake hacker aesthetics.
- Corporate SaaS gloss: stock gradients, glass cards, buzzword copy.

## Design Principles

1. Evidence over adjectives: every claim sits next to a live link, a stack or a date.
2. Calm confidence: restraint and precise detail signal skill more than spectacle.
3. Skimmable in a minute: hierarchy lets a recruiter reach projects and contact without effort.
4. Motion and 3D serve content: never block, delay or bury information; honor reduced motion.
5. Specific, not generic: no template patterns; each section earns its own structure.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: 4.5:1 body contrast, full keyboard navigation with visible focus, `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for all animation and 3D. Bilingual content (en-US, es-ES) must stay equally readable and not overflow in either language.
